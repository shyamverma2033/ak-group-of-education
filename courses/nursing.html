🩺 The Demand for Certified Nursing Professionals

Nursing is one of the highest-demand healthcare vocations globally. Qualified nurses with B.Sc Nursing or GNM certifications are eagerly recruited across top private multispecialty hospitals (Apollo, Fortis, Max, Medanta), central government hospitals (AIIMS NORCET), military nursing services, and international healthcare systems in the UK (NHS), Ireland, Canada, and Australia.

Ashutosh Kumar and A K Group of Education vet nursing colleges specifically for genuine hospital clinical bed affiliations. Nursing cannot be learned from textbooks alone; hands-on ICU, pediatric, and OT clinical rotations are mandatory for producing employable healthcare leaders.
