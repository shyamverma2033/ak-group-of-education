Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2025–2026 Academic Session

1. Information We Collect

When you fill out our counselling enquiry forms, contact us via WhatsApp, or call our office, we collect personal information you voluntarily provide, including: student name, parent/guardian name, mobile phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, academic marks, and entrance examination scores (NEET, JEE, etc.).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Evaluate admission eligibility and calculate potential college cutoffs.
  • Connect you directly with lead counsellor Ashutosh Kumar for personalized guidance.
  • Send relevant updates regarding official counselling schedules, choice-filling deadlines, and document requirements.

3. Zero Spam & No Third-Party Data Sale

We do NOT sell, rent, lease, or share your contact details with external third-party telemarketers or unauthorized call centers. Your details remain strictly with A K Group of Education.
