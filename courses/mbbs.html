MBBS Admission Counselling (India & Abroad)

Secure your medical doctor career through verified NEET UG counselling in India (AIQ, Deemed, State Quotas) or government-recognized medical universities abroad compliant with the National Medical Commission (NMC) FMGL Gazette.

Regulatory Body NMC / WHO / WDOMS

🩺 Course Overview

The Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS) is India's most prestigious undergraduate medical degree. Every year, over 20 lakh aspirants compete for limited government and private medical seats across India.

At A K Group of Education, under the personal guidance of Ashutosh Kumar, we provide objective, data-driven counselling. We evaluate your NEET score against historical opening/closing trends across Central Universities, Deemed Universities, and State Private Colleges, as well as affordable, high-reputation government universities abroad.

🗺️ Step-by-Step Admission Process

For MBBS in India

  1. NEET Qualification: Score at or above the qualifying percentile.
  2. MCC Online Registration (15% AIQ & 100% Deemed): Register on mcc.nic.in, deposit refundable security caution money, and submit curated choice preference order.
  3. State Quota Counselling (85% State Seats): Register on state counselling portals (e.g., BCECEB in Bihar, UPDGME in UP, KEA in Karnataka).
  4. Seat Allotment: Verify provisional allotment letter and complete document reporting at the designated nodal center or college campus.

For MBBS Abroad (NMC Compliant)

  1. University Selection: Select universities offering a minimum 54-month academic course + 12-month internship entirely in the English medium.
  2. Document Apostille & Translation: Attestation of 10th/12th marksheets by the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA).
  3. Admission Letter & Visa Invitation: Official invitation processing from the Ministry of Education of the host country.
  4. Student Visa & Pre-Departure Briefing: Visa stamp, forex card guidance, hostel allocation, and travel assistance.
Need help with Choice Filling? A single error in preference order can lead to losing an eligible seat. Ashutosh Kumar provides personalized preference matrices tailored to your rank.

📑 Mandatory Documents Required

  • Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card / Voter ID / Passport
  • 8 to 12 Passport Size Photographs (matching NEET application)
  • Domicile Certificate & Category/Caste Certificate (if claiming quota)
  • Valid Passport with at least 18 months validity (for Abroad applicants)
  • Migration & School Leaving Certificate (CLC / TC)
