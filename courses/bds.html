🦷 About the BDS Degree

Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) is the recognized medical qualification for dental practice, oral surgery, and orthodontics in India. For students whose NEET scores fall slightly below the cutoff for government MBBS, BDS offers an exceptional medical career with immediate private clinic setup potential, government hospital postings, and MDS specialization pathways.

A K Group of Education assists students in selecting colleges with proven daily clinical patient footfall. In dentistry, real clinical patient exposure during clinical postings is the single most vital factor for becoming a competent dental surgeon.

⚙️ Counselling & Seat Allotment Flow

  1. NEET Result Announcement: Note down your All India Rank (AIR) and Category Rank.
  2. Registration on Central & State Portals: MCC for Deemed Universities, State Counseling Portals (such as BCECEB Bihar, KEA Karnataka, UP NEET) for private and state quota dental seats.
  3. Choice Filling: Ashutosh Kumar assists you in creating an optimized dental college preference list based on OPD patient statistics and fee viability.
  4. Physical Verification & Admission: Submission of original certificates, DD of college tuition fee, and hostel allotment.
