💻 Choosing the Right Engineering Branch & College

Engineering remains the cornerstone of modern industrial and digital economies. However, not all engineering colleges deliver equal campus placement results. With hundreds of colleges mushrooming, selecting an institution with active corporate placement drives, modern labs, coding incubation hubs, and industry partnerships is crucial.

Under Ashutosh Kumar's guidance at A K Group of Education, we help students analyze verified on-campus median packages, branch-specific faculty quality, and coding culture rather than relying on promotional brochures.

📋 Eligibility Criteria

  • Passed 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics, and one optional subject (Chemistry, Computer Science, Biology).
  • Minimum 45% aggregate in PCM (40% for reserved category candidates).
  • Valid scorecard in JEE Main or respective State CET (WBJEE, COMEDK, BCECE, etc.) or qualifying 12th merit.
