A K Group of Education is an independent private educational consultancy providing professional guidance, counselling, and administrative documentation assistance. We are NOT a government counselling authority and do not represent the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC), National Testing Agency (NTA), Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (BCECEB), or any other regulatory body.

1. No Seat Guarantee / No Capitation Fees

We do not guarantee government seats, backdoor quotas, or unauthorized management allotments. All admission offers are strictly contingent upon students qualifying respective entrance examinations, fulfilling statutory eligibility, and receiving genuine allotment letters through authorized university or counseling committee channels.

2. Educational Counselling Scope

Our services include profile evaluation, historical cutoff analysis, document checklist verification, online choice-filling strategy, and admission procedural facilitation. Tuition fees of universities are always remitted directly by students and parents to official institutional bank accounts.

3. Accuracy of Information

While we make every effort to provide up-to-date information regarding cutoffs, fee structures, and regulatory guidelines (such as the NMC FMGL regulations for foreign medical graduates), regulatory bodies frequently amend guidelines. Students and parents are advised to cross-check final notices on official statutory portals.
