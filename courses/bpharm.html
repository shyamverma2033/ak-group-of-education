Pharmaceutical Sciences

B.Pharm & D.Pharm Admission Counselling

Step into the thriving global pharmaceutical industry. Guidance for PCI (Pharmacy Council of India) certified institutes for retail drug licenses, drug manufacturing, QA/QC, and government drug inspector careers.

Duration 4 Years (B.Pharm) / 2 Yrs (D.Pharm)
Eligibility 10+2 with Science (PCB or PCM)
Regulatory Approval PCI & AICTE Recognized

💊 Why Choose Pharmacy?

India is renowned as the "Pharmacy of the World". Whether your ambition is to start a retail medical agency/pharmacy, join multinational pharmaceutical companies (Cipla, Sun Pharma, Dr. Reddy's), or qualify for prestigious Government Drug Inspector examinations, B.Pharm and D.Pharm provide immediate licensing and career growth.

Under Ashutosh Kumar's mentorship at A K Group of Education, we ensure you enroll only in colleges having valid Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) approval. Unrecognized colleges without PCI approval cannot grant you a pharmacist registration certificate!

📋 Eligibility Comparison

CourseDurationEligibilityKey Outcome
B.Pharm (Bachelor of Pharmacy) 4 Years (8 Semesters) 10+2 with PCB or PCM (Min 45–50%) Registered Pharmacist, Drug Inspector eligibility, R&D, Clinical Trials, M.Pharm/MBA pathways.
D.Pharm (Diploma in Pharmacy) 2 Years (Annual system) 10+2 with PCB or PCM Rapid retail chemist drug licensing, hospital pharmacist postings in private/govt clinics.
