💻 BCA Overview: Practical Software & IT Skills

Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) is the most versatile 3-year undergraduate degree for students who wish to launch a career in information technology, web development, data analysis, and software services without spending four years on engineering.

📋 BCA Eligibility & Admission Flow

  • Qualification: Passed 10+2 / Intermediate in any stream (Science, Commerce, or Arts) with minimum 45–50% marks.
  • Mathematics Requirement: While some universities require Maths/Computer in 12th, many UGC-recognized universities offer bridge courses for Commerce and Arts students.
  • Admission Process: Merit-based admission or university entrance test. A K Group assists with college selection and enrollment.
